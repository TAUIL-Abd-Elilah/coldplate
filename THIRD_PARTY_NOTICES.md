## Generated media

The narration in `demo/coldplate_submission.mp4` was synthesised with
[`edge-tts`](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts) (LGPL-3.0), listed in
`requirements-video.txt` as an optional, isolated build dependency. It is a
client for a Microsoft Edge text-to-speech service; it is not vendored, not
linked, and not imported by any code that runs during a normal review, so its
copyleft terms do not reach this repository's own Apache-2.0 sources.

Stated plainly, because it is the one licence question here we cannot close
from inside the repository: **the LGPL applies to the client, not to the
service's audio output, and we hold no written confirmation that the generated
voice audio may be redistributed in a prize submission.** Everything else in
the video — every frame, figure, number and caption — is generated by
`scripts/build_demo_video.py` from this repository's own committed results, and
the narration text is `DEMO_SCRIPT.md`, which is ours. A reviewer who wants a
narration-free artefact has the complete script, the captions
(`demo/coldplate_submission.en.srt`) and the poster, and the video regenerates
from source with a different voice by passing `--voice` to the builder.
